# Skill: Evidence-aware policy options

## Goal
Turn relevant article evidence into public-policy options without overstating certainty.

## Required options
- ENSO-aware seasonal pollution forecasting.
- Regional airshed-level planning across city/state boundaries.
- Climate-informed crop-calendar advisories.

## For every option include
1. The action proposed.
2. Why the article makes it relevant.
3. Supporting fact IDs.
4. Expected benefit stated cautiously, without invented numerical impact.
5. At least two trade-offs or implementation risks.
6. A note that this is a proposal for human decision-makers, not a tested result from the article.
